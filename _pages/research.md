<p class="page-lead">I use gravitational-wave signals, stellar oscillations, and high-energy emission to investigate the structure, composition, and dynamics of compact stars.</p>

<div class="research-grid">
  <article class="research-card">
    <p class="research-card__index">01 · Waves</p>
    <h2>Continuous gravitational waves</h2>
    <p>A rapidly rotating pulsar can emit persistent gravitational waves if it carries a non-axisymmetric mass quadrupole. I study how thermal mountains, magnetically confined polar mounds, and nuclear pasta can generate or sustain such deformations in accreting neutron stars.</p>
    <p class="research-card__question"><strong>Key question:</strong> What could a continuous-wave detection reveal about dense matter and mountain formation at pulsar magnetic poles?</p>
  </article>

  <article class="research-card">
    <p class="research-card__index">02 · Oscillations</p>
    <h2>Compact-star asteroseismology</h2>
    <p>Stellar oscillation modes probe matter at densities and pressures inaccessible to terrestrial experiments. I study <em>g</em>-, <em>f</em>-, and torsional modes in neutron stars, strangeon stars, and white dwarfs, using their spectra to investigate composition, crystallization, crust properties, and magnetar quasi-periodic oscillations.</p>
    <p class="research-card__question"><strong>Key question:</strong> Which features of an observed oscillation spectrum can distinguish competing models of compact-star interiors?</p>
  </article>

  <article class="research-card">
    <p class="research-card__index">03 · Accretion</p>
    <h2>X-ray pulsars and radiation hydrodynamics</h2>
    <p>I model matter accreting onto strongly magnetized compact stars and connect flow dynamics with observables. Current topics include accretion-column structure, neutrino emission from polar mounds, the thermal evolution of transient systems, and super-Eddington accretion flows.</p>
    <p class="research-card__question"><strong>Key question:</strong> How do radiation, plasma, and strong magnetic fields shape the emission from X-ray pulsars?</p>
  </article>
</div>

## Broader research

My broader work includes boson stars, strangeon and hybrid-star models, black-hole deformations, fast radio bursts, and gravitational-wave signatures of exotic compact objects. Across these topics, I combine analytical models with numerical calculations, primarily in Python and Mathematica.
